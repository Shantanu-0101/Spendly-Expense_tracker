Privacy policy

This policy explains how Spendly, a personal expense-tracking application, handles your information.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Information we collect

When you create an account, Spendly collects details such as your name, email address, and password. When you use the service, Spendly stores the expense records you enter, including amount, category, date, and description.

How we use information

Spendly uses this information only to create and run your account, store and display your expenses, and show spending summaries. Your data is not used for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Your expense and account data is stored so the service can function. Spendly does not sell personal data. Data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service or where required by law.

Your choices

You can update your profile information and edit or delete your expense records at any time. You may stop using Spendly whenever you choose. You are responsible for keeping your login details private.

Changes to this policy

Spendly may update this policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Your continued use of the service after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
